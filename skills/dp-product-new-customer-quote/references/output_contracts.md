# Output Contracts

Choose exactly one contract before generation.

## Q01 Assessment + Quote Workbook

One Chinese `.xlsx` sheet containing confirmed student and coverage information, official assessment rows with source links and evidence status, final covered workload, authorized returned DP prices, verification time, and pending items.

## Q02 Standalone DP Quote

One concise quote page containing school, program, target year, package, covered scope, covered workload, original and discounted price only when returned by the quote service, and quote validity conditions.

Do not add proposal, planning, tutoring, sales, or fixed-value modules to either contract.
