# Official Research Policy

1. Use the user instruction date as the target calendar year unless the user specifies another academic year.
2. Prefer the target-year official university programme, module, handbook, calendar and assessment pages.
3. If target-year information is unavailable, use the latest currently published official university information and emit a year-mismatch warning.
4. Every course fact must retain source URL, source year and verification date. Student portal or user files are labeled separately from public official facts.
5. Consult `assets/data/official_source_registry.json` before searching. Reuse known official entry URLs, but revalidate facts whose year differs or whose verification is stale.
6. After verification, update the registry with the official school domain and useful programme/module/calendar entry URLs. Never cache dynamic access tokens or private portal URLs.
7. Search-engine snippets and third-party sites may help discovery but cannot be the final evidence when an official source exists.
