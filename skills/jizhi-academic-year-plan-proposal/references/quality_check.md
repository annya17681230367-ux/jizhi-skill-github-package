# Quality Check

Score out of 100 before final delivery.

## Checklist
- First draft is text/Markdown unless user explicitly requested final PDF/design render.
- Student/program/year details are customized, not generic.
- Official course sources are listed for every course where public data is available.
- Every course includes assessment components, weights, DDL/week, word count/duration where official sources provide them.
- Unknown assessment details are marked `待Canvas/Moodle/Assessment Brief/Rubric确认`, not written as facts.
- KPI numbers match the course cards and phase table.
- Course classification is explained.
- Six planning logic items appear: 个人学业画像, 问题诊断与定位, 目标差距分析, 课程优先级, 阶段行动建议, 学业风险清单.
- Service value, service content, and expected effect are explicit.
- Default方案 uses标准套餐 unless the user explicitly requested定制方案/定制课时.
- Standard package hours are consistent with the current questionnaire: 重点=6专业+10规划执行/陪跑, 非重点=2专业+8规划执行/陪跑, unless the questionnaire says otherwise.
- Every course has assessment focus, risk judgment, strategy, and hours.
- Stage dates are real or clearly marked as provisional.
- Annual path contains outputs, not only actions.
- Weekly loop is concrete enough for operations.
- AI智慧学习系统 is positioned as learning/tracking, not assignment代写.
- The phrase `AIAS` or `AI AS` is replaced with `AI智慧学习系统` unless the user explicitly requires the old product name.
- Full horizontal 极致AI logo is visible in the upper-left header of branded HTML/PDF outputs.
- IP images are used to fill whitespace or support sections, without covering text or making pages crowded.
- Overall background matches the IP palette: blue/cyan base, small purple accents, restrained yellow highlights.
- Parent-visible service value is explicit.
- No guaranteed grade/pass claims.
- Missing materials are listed.
- Footer states execution depends on actual Handbook/Moodle/Assessment Brief.
- Final confirmed proposal PDF was exported and visually checked before final delivery.
- Proposal and quote are separate final files unless the user explicitly asked to merge them.
- Proposal file contains no price, quote, discount, payment, amount, or promotion terms unless explicitly requested.
- Quote sheet is generated only after方案确认 or separate报价指令.
- Quote used both required pricing materials, preferably bundled pricing assets, not memory.
- Quote method is labeled: 标准套餐报价 / 门数折扣报价 / 定制方案报价 / 混合方案报价.
- Course-count/package discount comes from pricing files or explicit user instruction.
- Quote lists every course's service content and workload basis.
- Quote includes 原价, 折扣/优惠逻辑, and 折后价.
- Quote arithmetic was checked twice: course hours, monthly components, add-ons, original price, discount, and final price reconcile.

## Scoring
- 90-100: sendable client proposal.
- 80-89: strong draft, minor data gaps.
- 70-79: usable internal draft, needs course/date/hour confirmation.
- Below 70: do not present as client-ready.

If final quote or official course facts are missing, label proposal `初稿/待确认`, not final.
