# Planning Service Logic

Read this file only for course classification, execution rhythm or AI智慧学习系统 arrangements. Standard hours live only in `service_packages.yaml`.

## Course Classification

Classify a course as重点 when one or more material risks are present:

- high-weight exam, quantitative/software content, viva or difficult prerequisite;
- prior failure, clear student weakness or language barrier;
- dense DDLs, group dependency or complex assessment format;
- high target pressure or graduation progression risk.

Non重点 means the risk can be controlled through the standard planning loop; it does not mean the course is unimportant.

## Execution Rhythm

Use this fixed rhythm and customize only dates, courses and task names:

1. Daily: material synchronization, DDL/risk reminders and task status updates.
2. Weekly: specialist teaching, planning review, AI practice/tracking and next-week action list.
3. Monthly: progress summary, risk reclassification, hour/priority review and parent-visible feedback when applicable.
4. Stage review: pre-term, early term, assessment period, exam period and results review.

## AI智慧学习系统

Fixed positioning: `AI智慧学习系统是学生的可追踪学习空间。`

Its fixed functions are:

- course knowledge archive;
- concept practice and error records;
- progress and completion tracking;
- weak-point detection;
- review and oral/exam practice support.

Never describe it as an assignment-writing or substitution tool.

## Service Roles

- 学业规划师: planning, priority, DDL/risk tracking and stage review.
- 规划执行/陪跑课老师: task decomposition, follow-up, understanding checks and review.
- 专业课老师: course concepts, assessment logic and targeted practice.
- AI智慧学习系统: archive, practice, tracking and weak-point evidence.
