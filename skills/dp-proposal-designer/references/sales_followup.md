# DP Client Follow-Up Contract

Read only when the user requests WeChat or sales follow-up copy.

Use five short blocks:

1. One-sentence client situation.
2. Recommended DP product and why it fits.
3. Covered tasks and nearest execution stage.
4. Quote status: confirmed amount or `待报价确认`.
5. Materials and next action.

Do not include negotiation strategy, internal risk scores, margins or broad objection-handling scripts. Keep language factual and aligned with the generated proposal.
